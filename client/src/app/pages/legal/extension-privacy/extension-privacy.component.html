01Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the {{ extensionName }} browser extension (the “extension”) handles information. The extension is a companion to the {{ appName }} web application and is intended for authenticated {{ appName }} users who use it to carry out data-collection tasks they initiate. It works together with the {{ appName }} backend you or your organization connect it to; that backend is the destination for everything the extension collects.

02What the extension does

When a signed-in {{ appName }} user starts a social-media data-collection task, the extension runs that task inside the user’s own browser. It connects to the user’s {{ appName }} account, receives the platform and profile the user selected, opens the corresponding pages, extracts the requested information, and returns the results to the user’s {{ appName }} account. It has no other function, and it never acts on its own — only in response to tasks you start.

03Information we access and collect

Depending on the task you run, the extension may access and transmit the following to your {{ appName }} account:

Your {{ appName }} connection

The address of your {{ appName }} instance and the session token that authenticates the extension to your account. These are stored locally in the browser and sent only to your {{ appName }} instance so the extension can receive tasks and return results.

Public page content

The content of the public pages a task targets — for example a profile’s public videos, posts, media, and profile details — which the extension reads and parses in the browser and returns to your {{ appName }} account.

Authenticated platform sessions — only when you choose to sign in

Some tasks let you authorize collection by signing in to a platform yourself, in a window the extension opens for that purpose. When, and only when, you complete that step, the extension captures that platform’s session state — its cookies, local storage, session storage, IndexedDB data, the page origin, the signed-in username, and your browser’s user-agent string — and sends it to your {{ appName }} account so the task can run under your own signed-in session. If you do not perform this sign-in step, this data is not collected.

Existing site cookies

To let a task read the same pages you can already see while signed in, the extension may read the relevant site’s cookies for the specific hosts a task targets. This is used to access those pages on your behalf; it is not used to build any profile of your browsing.

The extension does not read pages, cookies, or storage for sites that are not part of a task you started, and it has no background tracking, analytics, or telemetry.

04How the information is used

  • To authenticate the extension to your {{ appName }} account and receive the tasks you start.
  • To open the pages a task targets and extract the information the task requested.
  • To run tasks under your own signed-in session when you have chosen to authenticate to a platform.
  • To return the collected results to your {{ appName }} account.

The information is used only to perform the tasks you initiate. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the extension’s single purpose.

05Where your data is sent and stored

All data the extension collects is sent only to the {{ appName }} instance you connect it to, over an encrypted connection, and is stored there under that instance’s own data-handling and retention terms. The extension does not send your data to any third party and does not operate its own separate servers. Your {{ appName }} connection address and session token are kept in the browser’s local extension storage on your device and are removed when you disconnect or remove the extension.

08Retention and your control

Data returned to your {{ appName }} account is retained and managed within that account. You control what runs and what is collected by choosing which tasks to start and whether to authenticate to a platform. You can disconnect the extension at any time, and removing it from your browser deletes its locally stored settings and connection details. To have data the extension returned removed, use your {{ appName }} account’s data controls or contact the operator of your {{ appName }} instance.

09Security

The extension communicates with your {{ appName }} instance over encrypted connections and keeps sensitive values, such as your session token, in local extension storage rather than transmitting them to any third party. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information in line with the {{ appName }} platform’s security practices.

10Children’s privacy

The extension is a professional tool intended for use by authenticated {{ appName }} users and is not directed to children. It is not intended for use by anyone under the age of majority in their jurisdiction.
