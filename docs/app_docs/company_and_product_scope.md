## Company Overview

Genesis Technologies is a software development and cybersecurity company that designs, develops, and maintains secure software solutions. The company delivers custom software development services and develops proprietary technology products for cybersecurity and cyber intelligence use cases.

The product in scope for this documentation set is Orion Intelligence. Orion Intelligence is a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) and Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) platform designed to collect, process, analyze, and visualize intelligence from multiple sources. It helps organizations perform cyber investigations, monitor digital threats, identify data breaches, and support intelligence-driven decision making.

Genesis Technologies combines software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and data analytics capabilities to build scalable, secure, and high-performance solutions for government organizations, enterprises, and private-sector clients.
